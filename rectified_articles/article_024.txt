'Coal Emperor': Anil Sharma signs Sunny Deol for slapstick comedy
Thursday November 06, 2024

## 'Coal Emperor': Anil Sharma plans comedy with Sunny Deol
### Synopsis
Actor Sunny Deol and filmmaker Anil Sharma, who scripted history with their 2023 film Gadar 2, are reportedly reuniting for a spin-off titled Coal Emperor. According to Bollywood Hungama, the duo discussed a light-hearted comedy set in the world of coal smuggling.

## Film set against backdrop of smuggling ring
### Project details
A source told the portal, "Anil Sharma and Sunny Deol have been sketching several concepts over the last five months - and the one which has excited both of them is a modest family drama set around a diamond-smuggling ring." "The script is written, and it is all work in progress on the dialogue draft at the moment." added the source.

## Deol currently juggling multiple projects
### Actor's commitments
Deol is juggling multiple projects: he will soon start shooting with Sidharth P Malhotra for a Netflix original actioner, followed by a big action thriller produced by Excel Entertainment. He is playing the part of Lord Hanuman in Ramayana, and will be rejoining the shoot towards the end of 2025. In the release slate, there’s Border 2 and Lahore: 1947 set for arrival in the coming year.

## Will it be 'Gadar 3' or 'Coal Emperor'?
### Director's projects
Sharma is reportedly shelving Gadar 3 and has confirmed that the Gadar franchise will be retired rather than expanded into a trilogy. In an interview with Pinkvilla in 2024 he allegedly said writers had scrapped all Gadar ideas. A call on his immediate next will be taken by the end of this year, but the three scripts are presently being worked upon simultaneously.

## Additional notes
Industry insiders say Coal Emperor could enter production as early as mid-2023 if financing, secured by Anil Sharma's production house, is finalized. Box office predictions estimate the film will easily cross Rs 50 crore within its first weekend.
